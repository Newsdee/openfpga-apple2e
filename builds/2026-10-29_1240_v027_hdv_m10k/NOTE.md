## What this build adds vs the 2105 build (2026-10-27, .a2k)
- **First bitstream with HDV read-only (H1–H3)**: `hdd.v` + `hdv_bridge.sv`
  + top-level slot-7 decode, `m_pd_mux` priority, CPU_WAIT, datatable
  poller. HDV slot (data.json "hard disk"), mount detect, no-device 0x28,
  protect 0x2B, WOZ under the mux. Read-only (0x0184 write arm present but
  `hdd_protect=1` inert).
- **Sector RAM is now an explicit `altsyncram` M10K** in the
  probe-verified 1-cycle configuration (`docs/REVIEW_RESULTS_HDD_2CYCLE_2026-10-29.md`):
  unregistered outputs on both ports (1-cycle reads), port A same-cycle
  writes, port B registered 2-stage writes (interface-identical to the
  donor's NBA). Fit line confirms:
  `True Dual Port; 512×8; 512×8; A InReg yes/OutReg no; B InReg yes/OutReg
  no; 4096 bits implemented; M10K_X11_Y23_N0` — the 512×1 pruning from the
  10-28 compile is gone (root cause was the 1-bit `hdd_dout`, fixed here).
- **Two real-bug fixes** from the expert review round:
  1. top-level `hdd_dout` was 1 bit (truncated 8-bit `D_OUT`) — every
     C0F0–C0FF/C700–C7FF response had dead bits 7:1 on hardware.
  2. `hdv_bridge` walker `hdd_ram_we` was permanently high at idle
     (unrequest-qualified `cnt_i < 512`) — clobbered sector_buf[0] every
     14M cycle.
